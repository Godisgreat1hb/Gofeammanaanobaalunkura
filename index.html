About Our Church

Discover our history, divine calling, core beliefs, and values as a family of believers in Sidama, Ethiopia.

Our History

Gofe Guenut Evangelical Church (ጐፌ ገነት ወንጌላዊ ቤተክርስትያን) was established with a powerful vision to proclaim the gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers in sound biblical doctrine, and serve the local community with boundless love and compassion. Over the years, the Lord has sustained us through prayer, faithful stewardship, and steady spiritual growth.

Our Vision

To see transformed lives honoring God and impacting generations.

Our Mission

Making disciples through worship, fellowship, discipleship, and service.

Sidama, Ethiopia

Shining the light of Christ

Our Beliefs

We believe in the Holy Trinity, the divine inspiration of the Holy Scriptures, salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, and the blessed hope of His return.

Core Values

Biblical Truth, Genuine Love, Prayerful Dependence, Servant Leadership, Integrity, and Active Community Engagement.

Spiritual Purpose

To glorify God by equipping believers for ministry, edifying the body of Christ, and evangelizing our local community and nations.
